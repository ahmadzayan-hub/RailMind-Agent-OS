Method and coverage

Transparent rules, inputs and exclusions for this planning indicator.

P25, P50 and P75 are quantiles of the observed spending-pace distribution used as planning scenarios; they are not statistical confidence intervals. Forecasts are never capped to the approved budget: a shortfall is shown as a funding gap and a decision required. This is not a time-series forecast. The source data contains cumulative expenditure and execution dates, but no monthly or quarterly spending history. Use it to prioritise review, then reconcile against approved cashflow, commitments and actual progress.
