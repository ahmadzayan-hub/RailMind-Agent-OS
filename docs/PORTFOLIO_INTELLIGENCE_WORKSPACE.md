# Portfolio Intelligence Workspace

Status: **bounded optional module, synthetic demo**. Portfolio Intelligence is a read-only decision-support view inside the Control Tower. It is not permission to turn RailMind into a general finance or project-portfolio system; finance systems remain authoritative.

## Data

The public repository and every public deployment carry a **seeded synthetic portfolio** produced by `src/portfolio/synthetic.ts` (30 initiatives, generic portfolios and business areas, no organisational totals, budgets, vendors, project names, contract references or source identifiers). Every record is classified `PUBLIC_SYNTHETIC`. The portfolio service refuses any other classification in a public build (`assertPublicClassification`). An internal deployment may inject a private provider behind the same `PortfolioDataProvider` port; its records would be classified `INTERNAL`, `CONFIDENTIAL` or `RESTRICTED` and never reach a public build.

The previous embedded snapshot cells were retired. `scripts/check-portfolio.mjs` fails the build if any of their aggregate fingerprints reappear, if a restricted identifier appears, if the page loads an external script, or if the static pack drifts from the service.

## Delivery

`GET /api/portfolio` returns the DTO (cells by portfolio, business area and award status; forecast metadata; totals; funding-gap exceptions). `web/portfolio.js` renders it live when the viewer is signed in with a portfolio scope, and otherwise renders `web/data/portfolio-pack.js`, generated by the same service. The page carries a permanent SYNTHETIC DEMO watermark and a provenance line (classification, provider, mode, model version, as-of date, calibration sample and confidence).

## Forecast governance

Model `pace-quantile-v2` (`src/forecast/budget-forecast.ts`):

- Calibration uses awarded, in-flight initiatives with recorded expenditure and at least 10% schedule progress. Initiatives whose execution end is on or before the as-of date are excluded; every exclusion is recorded with its reason. Regression tests cover this.
- P25, P50 and P75 are quantiles of the observed schedule-adjusted spending-pace distribution used as planning scenarios. They are not statistical confidence intervals.
- Sample size drives a confidence label: ADEQUATE (8 or more), LOW_CONFIDENCE (3 to 7), INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE (fewer than 3, forecast withheld).
- Projected cumulative expenditure is bounded by the total estimated cost, never by the approved budget. Each scenario reports remaining approved allocation and expected funding gap; the central-scenario gap is exposed as a management exception with a decision required. Committed and uncommitted allocation are reported where a committed amount exists.
- Backtesting (`backtest`) returns MAPE, bias and P25 to P75 band coverage against actuals; `ForecastDriftMonitor` records observations and flags drift beyond tolerance; `recalibrationMetadata` records model version, calibration date, sample size and exclusions. Every forecast carries provenance (model version, as-of, horizon, calibration sample, scenario semantics).

## Limitations

The source shape is cumulative expenditure and execution dates, not a monthly series, so this is not a time-series forecast. Use it to prioritise review, then reconcile against approved cashflow, commitments and actual progress under finance controls.
